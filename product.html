Panama Cloth Service Jacket

$398.00

Our Service Jacket returns—faithful to the P-41 Marine Corps fatigues that inspired it. The P-41 was built for extremes: sustained heat, hard use, and mobility. And so those demands shaped the fabric we chose for this warm weather edition.

This edition of The Service Jacket is constructed from a premium Japanese cotton Panama cloth — woven in a 2x2 basketweave structure where paired warp and weft threads interlace to create a fabric that is simultaneously open and stable. That structure is what gives this jacket its character and unrivaled breathability. Our mill in Okayama has refined this construction for generations, producing a cloth with exceptional yarn consistency and a finish that holds up while softening and deepening with every wear.

Details are where they matter: deep patch pockets sized for a notebook, double-needle stitching throughout, bartacks at every stress point, Corozo buttons built to last, and a collar that stands against wind and weather when you need it. Cut and sewn in New York City by one of our most trusted garment makers, the Panama Cloth Service Jacket is a piece you'll be hard-pressed to replace.

Care

Our Service Jacket has been rinsed and comes pre-shrunk.

Machine wash cold with similar colors. Use mild detergent and avoid bleach. For optimal longevity, turn inside out before washing. Line dry to maintain structure and prevent shrinkage. Iron on medium heat if needed.
