Spark Brand Identity

2026

Spark came to us growing fast and ready to let go of cookie-cutter design—a business in the flow that wanted its own flavor stitched into every surface it touches.

We built the identity from the ground up: a mark and type system with room to stretch, packaging that earns a place on the shelf, and signage that carries the brand out into the world. Every piece was designed to work as hard on a tote bag as it does on a storefront flag.

The result is a tailor-made system—not a template—that lets Spark show up consistently everywhere, and stand out while doing it.
